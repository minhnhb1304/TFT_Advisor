# VI Leveling Rules (ĐTCL)

Scope: leveling / econ / HP / roll-down rules per plan, from Vietnamese-language guides only. 5 iterations, ~14 searches, ~14 fetches.

## Bottom line

- VN guides are thin on numbers. Most are comp guides (CellphoneS, Hoàng Hà, FPT Shop) with 1-2 numeric rules each, often loose translations of EN content. No VN source gave a full standard curve table (4 at 2-1 / 5 at 2-5 / 6 at 3-2 / 7 at 4-1 / 8 at 4-2). That curve came up only in EN results (Mobalytics); EN sibling should cite it.
- Strongest VN-sourced numeric anchors:
  - Reroll 1-cost: stay at level 4-5, econ to 50 from 2-1 to 2-6, hyper roll at 3-1 if you already hold 4-5 copies; HP 60-80 is the "ideal" while doing it.
  - Reroll 2-cost: level 6 at 3-2 and roll.
  - Standard: level 8 at 4-2 or 4-5 (TFTPlus). Fast 8: level 8 "từ 4-1" and roll (CellphoneS, Black Rose).
  - Slow roll means keeping gold > 50. One roll-down floor: 32 gold.
  - Fast 9: never manual roll; HP > 40 at level 8 before pushing 9.
  - Eco/loss-streak (old Set 5.5): 50 gold + level 6 by Krugs (2-7); < 40 HP before 3-6, put units on board; > 50 HP at 3-6, keep econ'ing.
- No VN source gave a numeric win/loss-streak rule beyond "the streak bonus needs 50 gold" and "lose-streak in order to econ".

## Caveats

- Patch drift: the eco trắng and nguyên tắc articles are from 2021 (Set 5.5). The Wolves/3-7 tip comes from a Jan 2024 CellphoneS article (Set 10). The Black Rose article is 2025-02 (Set 13). TFTPlus is Set 12. Hoàng Hà and FPT Shop are Set 15-16. None are Set 18-specific.
- The TFTPlus "roll to 32" rule gave no stage. Treat it as a general roll-down floor, not a per-plan rule.
- VN sources rarely separate "standard" from "fast 8". They say "lên 8 ở 4-1/4-2" loosely.

## Unresolved questions

- No VN source for the full standard curve (4@2-1, 5@2-5, 6@3-2, 7@4-1, 8@4-2). The EN sibling needs to cover it; Mobalytics came up in search but I did not fetch it (EN, out of my scope).
- No numeric HP rule for standard or fast 8 in VN sources (e.g. "roll at 4-1 if HP < X").
- No VN rule for when a win-streak means you should level early (e.g. level 5 at 2-1 when streaking).
- VN streamer YouTube descriptions and Facebook groups were not indexed by search, so not covered.
