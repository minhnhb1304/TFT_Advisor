# ZH Sources: TFT Leveling, Econ, HP and Roll-Down Rules by Plan

Researcher: zh agent | 5 iterations | Chinese sources only | 2026-11-13

## Summary

- The Chinese baseline "正常运营" schedule is quoted the same way in many places: **2-1 四级, 2-5 五级, 3-2 六级, 4-1 七级, 4-5 八级, 5-5 九级** (兔子解说, gamersky, 2020). Newer comp guides (2022-2024) move level 8 earlier, to **4-2**.
- 赌狗 (reroll) rules depend on the carry's cost, not on a single schedule. 1费: "3-1 直接搜光". 2费: "3-2 搜全阵容2星, 4-2 搜3星主C". Slow-roll variant: "6人口卡50利息追3星".
- 速9 / 找大哥 schedule: **2-3 五级, 2-7 六级, 3-5 七级, 4-1 八级, 4-5 九级, 最迟 5-3 九级** (gamersky 2020).
- HP lines that a source actually backs: **30 血 = 容错线** (two late losses). **低于 50 血 at stage 4 ≈ 死亡** (10.3 patch). **<40 血 → 不上8/不上9, 继续D质量**. **60+ 血 → 可存钱上8/上9**. **40 血下被迫拉7** (doubles mode).
- Streaks: "能连胜一定不要连败". Avoid alternating win/loss. Streak matters less after 3-1/3-2 because players are near 50 gold by then.
- Caveat: most fetched guides are 2020-2022 (S3-S7). XP costs and shop odds have changed since. Treat the numbers as **heuristic anchors**, not set-18 truths.

## Conflicts with Western Advice

- **The Chinese "standard" puts L8 at 4-5. The Western standard in recent sets puts it at 4-2.** Newer CN comp guides (2022+) also say 4-2, so the 4-5 figure is old (S3-S4). For current patches, prefer 4-2.
- **L5 timing.** CN gives 2-5 (with 2-3 if there is PvE gold). Western advice often levels to 5 at 2-5 as well, so these match.
- **速9 at 4-5 / latest 5-3 (CN 2020)** is earlier than the Western "fast 9", which typically goes 4-1 L8 → 5-1 or 5-2 L9. Not verified for set 18.
- **The CN HP rules (<40 roll, 60+ save) are stated more explicitly** than Western heuristics. They are also old (10.3 patch) and repeated in a later snippet.
- **The CN community distrusts 速8.** "不要速八" is comp-specific but representative. Western sources treat fast 8 as the default for 4-cost carries.

## Unresolved Questions

- No verified source for set 18 / 2025-2026 specifically. Bilibili 专栏 (cv44995427, opus/840312628062453825) were captcha-blocked, and 知乎 / NGA / 小黑盒 were not reached. Leveling cost changes in later sets (e.g. 4-1 L7 vs 4-2 L8 XP costs) are not reflected.
- The "<40 HP stay 8, ≥60 go 9" and "≥50 HP for 速9" quotes are snippet-only. I could not find the origin page.
- No CN source gives an exact "roll down to X gold" number for standard 4-2 beyond "8人口后花20到30块D" (snippet), "7级剩30块" and "最少留10/20块".
